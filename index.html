About Us

Studio — Practice

A small studio with a settled point of view.

Today Would Be Nice is a design studio in Lahore, Pakistan. We design prototypes, applications, and websites for founders, product teams, and organisations who care how things are made.

Our work is rooted in the Swiss tradition — clear typography, considered grids, and ideas placed deliberately on the page. We believe restraint is not the absence of style; it is the style.

We work in small engagements with full attention. No filler, no factory. Just the design, well made.
